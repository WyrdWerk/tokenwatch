How the open model finder works

Each use case has an assumed token mix, required capabilities (such as tool calling, structured output, and a minimum context window), benchmark weights, and an absolute quality floor. Only open-weight models are considered unless you include proprietary models. Best quality is the highest benchmark composite; best value sits on the quality-versus-price Pareto frontier rather than a score-per-dollar ratio; cheapest good-enough is the cheapest model that clears the floor. Providers are ranked on blended price, time to first token, throughput, and uptime, and a close call means the top two are within three points. Creative writing is led by LMArena preference ratings (CC BY 4.0); chat also shows a people’s favourite.
